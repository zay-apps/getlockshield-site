FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Do I need Shopify Plus?

No. LockShield works on every Shopify plan, from Basic to Plus. Shopify's own B2B features are for Plus only; LockShield gives you price hiding and wholesale access without upgrading.

Can someone still buy a locked product with a direct link?

No. LockShield adds a checkout rule that runs on Shopify's servers. If a locked product is in the cart, checkout is refused until the customer logs in with the right access or removes it.

Does it change my theme code?

No. LockShield uses a Shopify theme app extension that you turn on with one switch in the theme editor. Nothing is written into your theme, so uninstalling leaves it exactly as it was.

How do wholesale customers get access?

You choose: any logged-in customer, customers with a tag such as wholesale or VIP, an access code, or a secret link. You can combine several conditions on one lock.

What's included in the free plan?

One lock on up to three products, with checkout protection and a small "Protected by LockShield" badge. Upgrade any time to lock unlimited products, collections, pages or your whole store, with a 15-day free trial.

Which languages does it support?

The app and the messages your customers see are available in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
